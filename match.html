Logistics

Step.1

Register your information here

If you want to be both a tutor and tutee, please register twice!

Step.2

Browse through our match sheet. If you find a tutor/tutee you'd like to reach out to, please send an email from your student email to --- with the name of the tutor/tutee.

Step.3

Our staff will contact the person ASAP, usually within 24h.

If that person also agrees to connect, then our staff will help you exchange emails, and you two can discuss further details.

If that person refuses to connect, then our staff will also let you know, and no information will be shared.

Notes

You're very welcome to reach out to other people if you didn't match the first time!

If you no longer want to be matched, please email --- to either have your information deleted or marked as paused.
